How to introduce your application

“I have 2 years and 7 months of adult Cardiac ICU experience and DOH eligibility. I am interested in the ICU nursing recruitment route. Could you confirm whether I meet the licensing requirements and which application reference you need?”

Do not share account passwords, one-time codes or patient details. A profile number and a job application reference may be different. Check the actual portal record rather than guessing.

Questions to clarify

  • Is this Dubai Hospital specifically, and which ICU service?
  • Have I been shortlisted, and for which date and time?
  • Where is the confirmed interview venue?
  • Is DOH eligibility accepted at application stage?
  • Which documents and reference number should I bring?
